### Welcome to the unbeknownst page of this site. 

I was born and raised in Dalian, a coastal city in northern China. By the way, it’s a lovely place, so consider visiting sometime if you aren’t on a tight, intensive business trip. I spent my entire childhood there until I flew to Britain for university. That was the moment I started making CupcakKe remixes, which I still consider my biggest achievement so far. So thank you all for making my work a part of online queer culture.

I earned my Bachelor’s degree in Communication and Media and my Master’s in Promotional Media from the University of Leeds. Despite being interrupted by strikes and industrial actions, I truly value every single minute I spent living there. Looking back, I gained something that no one can ever take away.

I made the decision to become a PR practitioner while studying for my Master's, which is why I joined Burson and Ruder Finn. That is how my life slowly, slowly became ... like ... a plane on an endless excursion, failing to land on the runway, or a pillow being thrown, bitten, and torn apart by a cat. I still fantasise about what would have happened if I hadn't chosen agencies over in-house teams ... if one thing had been different, would everything be different today?

But there are no "what ifs." In fact, you can’t expect life to be a straight line pointing at the sun, rushing toward the finish line. I’ve been having a hard time adjusting. Hopefully it pays off.

Because life ends when it ends.
